# 𓆩♡𓆪 Activity 1: Presentation Design Principles

## 𓇢𓆸 Activity Description

This activity focused on applying the basic principles of presentation design through the creation of a promotional poster. Using the elements, text, images, and format provided by our professor, I developed a **Free Coffee Poster** by arranging and combining the given materials into a visually appealing design. I applied proper font selection, readability, alignment, spacing, and organization to make the poster clear, attractive, and easy to understand.

## 🦢 What Have I Learned?

Through this activity, I learned how important design principles are in creating an effective and visually appealing poster. I understood that choosing readable fonts, maintaining proper alignment, and organizing text and images help make information easier to follow. One challenge I encountered was arranging the provided elements in a way that looked balanced without making the poster appear too crowded. I addressed this by adjusting the placement, spacing, and size of the different elements. Moving forward, I can apply these principles to create better presentations, posters, and other digital materials that communicate information clearly while maintaining an appealing design.
